Shape Valley is out now

Shape Valley is a calm, low-stimulation driving game for kids aged 6–8 on iPhone and iPad. Soft shapes, quiet sounds, no timers, no scores and no ads. It's out now on the App Store.

The four calm games

  • Calm Drive: free driving through soft hills.
