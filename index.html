Calm little drives for little drivers

Four gentle driving games for children, built from soft, simple shapes. Nothing flashes, beeps or rushes them along.

Coming soon to the App Store

Calm Drive

Wander quiet roads past giant domes, pyramids and arches. No goals, no rush.

Taxi

Pick up passengers and drive them home, following glowing rings.

Tidy Dozer

Push the toys into the hole, with a springy boing when you need it.

Lawnmower

Ride the mower and turn tall grass into neat stripes.

Made with parents in mind

  • Parents set play time, speed and time of day
  • Games end calmly, never "game over"
  • Auto drive for the youngest drivers
  • Grown-up gate on purchases and settings
  • No ads, no tracking, no accounts
  • Works offline on iPhone and iPad
