Privacy Policy

Shape Valley: Tiny Drivers · Last updated 23 October 2026

Shape Valley is made for young children, so it is built to collect as little as possible: no personal information at all.

What we never collect

  • No names, email addresses, photos, voice, location or contacts. There are no accounts.
  • No analytics, advertising, advertising identifiers or tracking of any kind.
  • Nothing your child does inside the games leaves the device.

What stays on the device

Grown-up settings (such as play time, speed and time of day) and onboarding progress are saved on the device so the app remembers them. They never leave the device and are deleted with the app.

Subscriptions

Payments are handled by Apple through the App Store; we never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. To check which plan is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives the App Store purchase record and a random ID created by the app. That ID is not linked to your identity and is used only to provide your subscription, never for advertising or tracking. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for children and does not collect personal information from anyone, in line with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws. Purchases, links that leave the app and the Parent Zone sit behind a parental gate.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Launch list (website only)

Parents can choose to join our launch list on shapevalleygame.com/launch/. This is separate from the app.

  • What we collect: your email address only, plus your confirmation that you are a parent or guardian aged 18 or over and the date you signed up.
  • Why: to email you about the Shape Valley launch. We won't use it for anything else, and we never sell or share it.
  • Where it's stored: in a private Google Sheet in our Google account (Google is our storage provider; see Google's privacy policy).
  • Unsubscribe or delete: email zachlaudev@gmail.com and we'll remove your email promptly. Every launch email also tells you how to unsubscribe.
  • Children: the launch list is for adults only. We collect no information from or about children.

Signing up sets no cookies and adds no analytics or tracking.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
