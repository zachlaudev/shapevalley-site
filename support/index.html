Support

Questions, feedback or a problem? Email zachlaudev@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days.

How do I open the grown-up settings?

On the Home screen, tap the grown-up button and answer the short maths question. Inside a game, press and hold the gear for a moment.

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Open the plans screen and tap Restore, using the same Apple Account you subscribed with. Family plans are shared through Family Sharing.

How do I cancel?

In the Parent Zone, tap Manage subscription, or go to Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad.

Does the app need the internet?

No. All games work offline. Only buying, restoring and checking a subscription use the internet.
